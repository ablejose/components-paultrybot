PoultryBot — Component Datasheet

An Octopus-style autonomous ground robot for broiler houses. It drives across the litter to clean (aerate litter + fog biocide) and watch the flock (health, mortality, environment) — 24/7. This page is the parts reference: what each component is, why it is needed, and how it works.

Ground AMR Broiler / deep-litter Litter scarifier Biocide nebuliser Edge-AI surveillance Self-docking

01Overview

The design copies the working principle of the Octopus Biosafety ground robot (France): a low, self-recharging, all-terrain platform that continuously scarifies litter and nebulises disinfectant while mapping the barn environment — extended here with an edge-AI perception stack for mortality and health surveillance.

9

2

Constant cleaning (litter + disinfection) and constant surveillance (health + climate).

24V

LiFePO4 pack feeding a protected 24V bus with 12V / 5V rails for logic and sensors.

Dock-and-go patrols with autonomous recharge between cleaning/scan passes.

03Component datasheet

Grouped by subsystem. Each table: the component, an example part, why it exists on this robot, and how it works.

1 · Chassis & mobility

The body that survives a poultry house

A low, sealed, corrosion-proof platform that carries every module and can cross loose, uneven, damp litter without bogging down or letting dust and ammonia inside.

ComponentExample partWhy it is neededHow it works
Frame / chassisWelded 6061 aluminium or 304 stainless skeletonRigid mounting for all subsystems; must resist constant washdown, moisture and ammonia corrosion.Load-bearing skeleton; forces from wheels and scarifier transfer through it instead of flexing sensitive electronics.
Sealed shellIP65+ ABS/polycarbonate enclosure with gasketsKeeps dust, litter, moisture and disinfectant out of electronics; lets you pressure-wash the robot between flocks.Gasketed panels + cable glands form a continuous barrier; internal air kept at slight positive pressure by fan+filter.
All-terrain wheels4× 150–200 mm ribbed pneumatic/foam wheelsLarge contact patch floats over deep litter; ribs give traction on loose bedding so the bot does not dig in.Big diameter lowers ground pressure; tread grips the friable top layer to pull the chassis forward.
Compliant suspensionRocker mounts / spring dampers per wheelKeeps all wheels loaded on lumps and keeps cameras/LiDAR steady for clean data.Passive springs let each wheel move independently over bumps while the chassis stays level.
2 · Drive & motion control

Making it move — precisely and slowly

Differential drive gives torque at crawl speed (safe around birds) and turn-in-place agility. Encoders + IMU let the robot know where it is.

ComponentExample partWhy it is neededHow it works
Drive gearmotors2× 24V BLDC gearmotors, ~100–200 W, high reductionDeliver high low-speed torque to push through litter without stalling; brushless = long life, low maintenance.Electromagnet coils spin the rotor; the gearbox trades speed for torque so the bot crawls with force.
Motor driver / ESCODrive or VESC (dual-channel)Turns MCU commands into precise, current-limited motor power; protects motors from overload.Switches battery current to the motor coils with PWM; closed-loop control regulates speed/torque.
Wheel encodersQuadrature / hall encoders on each drive motorProvide odometry (distance & speed) for navigation and closed-loop driving.Count pulses as the shaft turns; pulse rate = speed, total count = distance travelled.
IMU (9-DOF)Bosch BNO085 / BMI270Gives heading and tilt; fused with odometry it corrects wheel slip on loose litter.MEMS accelerometer + gyroscope + magnetometer sense motion and orientation in 3 axes.
3 · Cleaning payload

The Octopus core: scarify + disinfect

The two functions that define an Octopus-style robot. Aerating litter keeps it dry and low-ammonia; nebulising biocide cuts the pathogen load and reduces antibiotic use.

ComponentExample partWhy it is neededHow it works
Scarifier drumRotating tine/rake drum + 24V motor & gearboxAerates and de-cakes litter → moisture evaporates, ammonia (NH3) generation drops, footpad health improves.Spinning tines dig into the top litter layer and lift/mix it with air as the robot drives forward.
Tool height actuator12V linear actuator, 50–100 mm strokeSets scarifying depth and lifts the drum clear for transit, docking and turns.Motor turns a lead-screw that extends/retracts the rod, raising or lowering the drum.
Biocide tank5–10 L food-grade HDPE tank + level sensorOnboard disinfectant reservoir for autonomous fogging runs; level sensor triggers refill alerts.Gravity/pump feed to the nozzles; float or capacitive sensor reports remaining volume.
Nebuliser pump + nozzlesDiaphragm/peristaltic pump + fine-mist (fogging) nozzlesLays down an even micro-droplet fog of biocide across litter and surfaces without soaking them.Pump pressurises the liquid; nozzles atomise it into fine droplets that settle uniformly.
4 · Perception & navigation

Eyes for driving and for surveillance

One sensor set does double duty: navigate safely around birds and equipment, and feed the AI models that detect dead birds, distribution and equipment faults.

ComponentExample partWhy it is neededHow it works
2D/3D LiDARRPLIDAR S2 / Livox Mid-360Primary navigation sensor for SLAM mapping and reliable obstacle detection in low light and dust.Fires laser pulses and times the reflections to measure distance in every direction → a live 2D/3D map.
Depth (stereo) cameraIntel RealSense D435 / Luxonis OAK-D3-D obstacle avoidance plus per-pixel depth for spotting birds, wet spots and objects on the floor.Two offset lenses compute disparity between images to derive distance for each pixel.
RGB cameraGlobal-shutter 1080p+ moduleFeeds vision AI: dead-bird detection, bird distribution, drinker/feeder faults, litter condition.CMOS sensor captures colour frames; global shutter avoids motion blur while driving.
Thermal cameraFLIR Lepton 3.5 moduleWorks in darkness; heat signatures help flag sick/dead birds and hot/cold micro-zones.A microbolometer array measures infrared radiation and maps it to a temperature image.
Proximity + bumpersToF/ultrasonic ring + contact bumper switchesLast line of collision safety against birds, walls and equipment if vision/LiDAR miss something.ToF/ultrasonic measure short-range distance; bumpers cut drive on physical contact.
5 · Environmental sensing

Mapping the air the birds breathe

Because the robot is on the floor, these readings are taken exactly where the birds live — far more meaningful than fixed ceiling sensors, especially for ammonia which pools low.

ComponentExample partWhy it is neededHow it works
Ammonia (NH3) sensorElectrochemical NH3 cell (0–100 ppm)NH3 is the #1 air-quality killer of growth and welfare; must be mapped at bird height.Ammonia reacts at an electrode producing a current proportional to concentration.
CO2 sensorNDIR CO2 module (e.g. SenseAir)Tells you whether ventilation is adequate; rising CO2 = under-ventilation.Measures how much infrared light CO2 molecules absorb at their characteristic band.
Temp + humiditySensirion SHT31/SHT45Thermal comfort drives FCR; humidity predicts litter caking and ammonia release.Capacitive/bandgap elements change with heat and moisture; digitised to °C and %RH.
Dust / PM sensorLaser scattering PM sensor (e.g. SPS30)Airborne dust is a respiratory hazard to birds and workers and clogs equipment.A laser illuminates passing particles; scattered light is counted to estimate mass/count.
Litter moisture probeCapacitive prong probe (rear-mounted)Wet litter is the root cause of ammonia, caking and footpad burn — measure it directly.Water changes the dielectric between the prongs; capacitance maps to % moisture.
Light + mic arrayAmbient lux sensor + MEMS microphone arrayVerify the lighting program; audio AI detects respiratory rales, sneezing and stress calls.Photodiode measures lux; multiple mics capture sound for ML classification + direction.
6 · Compute & control

Two brains: smart and safe

A GPU computer for heavy AI and mapping; a deterministic microcontroller for the fast safety loop. Splitting them keeps motion control reliable even while AI is busy.

ComponentExample partWhy it is neededHow it works
Edge-AI computerNVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano / NXRuns vision + audio neural nets and SLAM on-board, so decisions do not depend on the network.Its GPU runs many neural-network operations in parallel for real-time inference.
Real-time MCUSTM32F4/F7 or Teensy 4.1Owns motor, scarifier, pump and E-stop timing with guaranteed millisecond response.Runs a fixed control loop reading sensors and driving outputs deterministically.
Internal busCAN bus + USB3 + UARTReliable wiring between compute, MCU and sensors in an electrically noisy shed.CAN carries robust differential messages; USB3 handles camera/LiDAR bandwidth.
StorageNVMe SSD (256 GB+)Buffers video, maps and logs when the network is down; holds the AI models.Solid-state flash stores data locally; syncs to cloud when connected.
7 · Power system

Safe energy, all shift

LiFePO4 chemistry is chosen for safety and cycle life. A BMS protects the pack; DC-DC converters supply clean logic/sensor rails; an E-stop cuts motor power instantly.

ComponentExample partWhy it is neededHow it works
Battery pack24V LiFePO4, 20–40 AhHigh current for motors, hundreds of cycles, and far safer (no thermal runaway) than Li-ion in a barn.Stores energy in lithium-iron-phosphate cells and delivers it as stable DC.
BMS4S/8S battery management systemPrevents over/under-charge, over-current and over-temp; balances cells for long life & safety.Monitors each cell and opens a protection MOSFET if any limit is exceeded.
DC-DC converters24V→12V and 24V→5V buck modulesSensors and logic need clean, correct voltages the raw pack cannot provide.Switch-mode regulation steps the bus voltage down efficiently to each rail.
Charging dockAuto-dock with sprung contact padsEnables unattended 24/7 operation — the robot recharges itself between passes.The bot homes to the dock; contacts mate and the charger tops up the pack via the BMS.
E-stop + contactorMushroom E-stop + main power contactorInstant, hardware-level power cut for human and bird safety.Pressing the button physically breaks the motor-power circuit independent of software.

08Suggested build order

De-risk in stages: get it driving safely first, then add sensing, then the cleaning payload, then autonomy.

  1. Rolling chassis Assemble frame, wheels, motors, drivers, battery, BMS, E-stop. Goal: safe manual (RC) driving over litter.
  2. Motion control Add MCU, encoders, IMU; tune closed-loop crawl speed and turn-in-place.
  3. Perception Mount LiDAR + cameras on the Jetson; bring up SLAM mapping and obstacle avoidance.
  4. Environment sensing Add the gas/climate/moisture/mic pod; start logging bird-level maps to the dashboard.
  5. Cleaning payload Fit the scarifier drum + height actuator; then the tank, pump and fogging nozzles.
  6. Autonomy & docking Add the charging dock, homing beacon, human-detect safe-speed, and dock-and-go patrol scheduling.
  7. AI surveillance Deploy dead-bird / distribution / drinker-fault models and audio health detection; wire up alerts.
Start with surveillance + litter aeration (highest value, lowest risk). Add biocide fogging and any pickup manipulation last — they are the hardest and most safety-sensitive.
